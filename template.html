How full is Kyrgyzstan’s battery?

The Toktogul reservoir feeds the power plants that keep the lights on in winter. We measure its water surface from space with NISAR, the NASA and ISRO radar satellite that sees through clouds and in the dark.

    Watch the shoreline move

    Eleven radar images from the same orbit, 20 June to 30 October 2026. Water is the dark, smooth area. Drag the slider, or press play.

    The reservoir grew fastest at the end of June

    How we tell water from dry ground

    For this radar, calm water and dry, smooth ground are both very dark. One image cannot separate them. Rain can: wet ground turns bright, water does not change. Press “Show a dry day” and “Show a wet day” above to see it.

      What this page cannot tell you

        Data. NISAR Level-2 GCOV radar backscatter (L-band, HH and HV), NASA and ISRO, distributed by the Alaska Satellite Facility DAAC. Track . Products are provisional. Weather record for the wet days: NASA POWER daily precipitation (MERRA-2 and GEOS-IT). Official volumes: statements of the Ministry of Energy of the Kyrgyz Republic, reported by Economist.kg on 8 July and 19 October 2026. Winter water use: bne IntelliNews. Dead level and capacity: 24.kg.

        Code. The notebook that reads the data and makes every number on this page is open: GitHub repository. Run on simulated radar scenes with a known answer, the same code came within 1 km² of the true area; real ground is messier.
